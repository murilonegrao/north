# Source: Ireland immigration rules for students and their families

Desk research for the Irish country module. It covers English language courses (Stamp 2, at least 25 weeks) and higher education at every level. Requirements touched: BR-04 (filter), BR-07 (four cost figures), BR-08 (spouse work), BR-12 (dated rules table).

Date of the research: **2026-11-22**. Every fact below was read on that date on the cited page, unless the row says otherwise. ISD = Immigration Service Delivery (Department of Justice, Home Affairs and Migration), `irishimmigration.ie`.

## Summary

- **Family filter: Ireland fails it for almost every student.** Students "are typically not permitted to bring family members with them" (ISD student FAQ). Under the Non-EEA Family Reunification Policy of 12 June 2026, students are **ineligible sponsors**, except **PhD students** and students on ISD-approved scholarship programmes. Graduates on Stamp 1G are also ineligible. This applies to English language, bachelor's and master's students.
- **PhD is the exception, with limits.** A PhD student can be joined by a spouse or partner and minor children right away (Category B). The family gets **Stamp 2A**, which does **not allow work**. The PhD must be finished within 4 years, and progress is checked at each renewal. The child schooling rule is only half documented (see Family).
- **Money:** €10,000 a year for living costs (this is ISD's "estimated cost of living in Ireland for a student for one academic year"), or €833 a month (€6,665 for 8 months) for stays of 8 months or less. The fees must also be paid: in full if under €6,000, otherwise at least €6,000. **There is no amount per dependant**, because dependants are not foreseen.
- **Work:** 20 h/week in term; 40 h/week in June to September and from 15 December to 15 January.
- **Post-study:** Stamp 1G, 12 months after NFQ level 8, or 12 + 12 months after level 9 or above. Stamp 2 plus Stamp 1G time is capped at 7 years (8 years for level 9+).
- **Pending change:** the ILEP (programme list) is being replaced by QQI's **TrustEd Ireland** list. ILEP "will continue in operation until the decisions from the second TrustEd application are announced which is scheduled for the end of 2026".

## 4. Family

### 4.1 Students on Stamp 2 (English, bachelor's, master's)

- ISD-FAQ: "Can I bring family members with me? **No, students are typically not permitted to bring family members with them.** There are some very exceptional circumstances where this may be permitted."
- ISD-FR and ISD-FRT, "Ineligible Sponsors": "Those in Ireland as students (except for those listed in Category B)" and "Graduates under the Third Level Graduate Programme".
- ISD-FRP §5.4: "Those resident in Ireland as students (except for PhD students and those on certain recognised scholarship programmes) or on a Third Level Graduate Scheme permission are not eligible to sponsor an application under this Policy."
- ISD-DEG (Family Reunification): "In general students will continue to have no rights to Family Reunification." A spouse or child "will be considered as applicants in their own right but cannot advance their application for entry or residence on the basis of their relationship to another student". "Students will not be permitted to be accompanied or joined by children other than those born during their stay." Visits by children: "In general no".
- ISD-DEG lists exceptions: (a) PhD (NFQ 10), finished within 4 years; (b) a programme agreed between Ireland and another state; (c) **private means** "at a level that clearly allows them to support themselves and their family without recourse to public funds and without engaging in employment", plus any immigration levy for children; (d) a child's short visit. Item (c) still shows in ISD-STAMPS ("Stamp 2A may also be granted to spouses of certain financially independent students in limited circumstances"). **No amount and no procedure are published.** The 2026 family policy does not list it as a sponsor category.
- What this means: a spouse can come only as a person in their own right, for example as a student on their own Stamp 2. Children cannot accompany a Stamp 2 parent.

### 4.3 Researchers, employment permit holders and graduates

| Status of the main applicant | Can the family come? | Family permission | Can the spouse work? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Stamp 1G graduate** (Third Level Graduate Programme) | **No**, ineligible sponsor | — | — | ISD-FR, ISD-FRP §5.4 |

Conflict: DETE-CSEP still says that spouses of CSEP holders "apply … for a Dependant/Partner/Spouse Employment Permit". ISD-STAMPS and ISD-FRP (June 2026) say Stamp 1G with no permit. The **ISD policy is newer and is the one that rules immigration status.** Use ISD.

Which policy version applies (ISD-FRA): applications from 2026-06-12 → June 2026 policy; from 2025-11-26 to 2026-06-11 → November 2025 policy; earlier → 2016 policy.

**Not verified:** whether a funded PhD candidate can be on a Hosting Agreement (which gives the spouse Stamp 1G) instead of Stamp 2 (which gives Stamp 2A). The scientific researcher visa page only says that family members apply separately. It depends on the research organisation; ask the institution.

## Open questions

1. Does the levy for children of students in state schools exist today, and what does it cost? Only the 2011 guideline (republished 2024) mentions it. Ask the Department of Education or an international office in writing.
2. The "private means" exception for families: is it still operated, and at what amount? It is not in the 2026 family policy. Only ISD-STAMPS hints at it (Stamp 2A for spouses of financially independent students).
3. Can a funded PhD be on a Hosting Agreement (spouse on Stamp 1G, can work) instead of Stamp 2 (spouse on Stamp 2A, no work)? This is per institution. Ask the international or research office.
